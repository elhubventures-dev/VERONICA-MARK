# Brand Voice & Messaging

**Source:** Brand Guidelines Volume I (Foundation) + Volume III (Voice & Messaging)

## Tagline

**Curated for the Exceptional.**

Use on: homepage, About, packaging, shopping bags, campaigns, email signatures, corporate presentations.  
Do **not** glue the tagline to the logo in every UI chrome instance.

## Voice

Speak with quiet confidence. Refined, never flashy. Warm, never overly casual. Knowledgeable, never complicated.

Tone attributes: Professional · Elegant · Warm · Confident · Knowledgeable · Authentic · Refined  

Avoid: arrogance, exaggeration, overly casual slang, hype.

## Preferred vs avoid

**Prefer:** Curated, Exceptional, Authentic, Timeless, Craftsmanship, Refined, Premium, Exclusive, Elegant, Luxury  

**Avoid:** Cheap, Bargain, Crazy Sale, Massive Discount, Best Ever, Lowest Price  

## Product description structure

1. Luxury introduction  
2. Product highlights  
3. Materials or fragrance notes  
4. Benefits  
5. Usage guidance  
6. Authenticity assurance
