
VERONICA MARK
Brand Guidelines — Volume II
Visual Identity System
Logo System
Primary Logo• Custom VM Monogram + VERONICA MARK wordmark• Primary usage on website, packaging, documents and marketing.Secondary Logo• Wordmark only for narrow spaces and editorial layouts.Icon-only Logo• VM monogram for app icon, favicon, authentication seal, social avatars and embossing.Minimum clear space: equal to the width of the 'V' stem.Minimum digital size: 24px icon, 120px full logo.
Official Brand Colors
Royal PurpleHEX: #4B246ALuxury CreamHEX: #F8F4ECCharcoal BlackHEX: #1A1A1AChampagne GoldHEX: #C7A25AUse these values consistently across all digital and print materials.
Color Usage
65% Luxury Cream25% Royal Purple10% Charcoal Black and Champagne Gold accentsPurple is the signature brand color.Gold is reserved for premium highlights and luxury finishes.
Typography
Primary Display: Playfair DisplayPrimary UI: InterSupporting Typeface: ManropeHeadings should use elegant spacing and restrained weight.Body text should prioritize readability.
Logo Usage Rules
Always preserve proportions.Never stretch, rotate or distort.Never change official colors.Never place on busy backgrounds without sufficient contrast.Prefer cream or white space around the logo.
Monogram Usage
The VM monogram is the universal recognition symbol for VERONICA MARK.Approved uses:• Mobile App Icon• Website Favicon• Social Media Avatar• Product Authentication Seal• Packaging Foil Stamp• Loyalty Cards• Gift Boxes• Watermarks
Brand Pattern
Develop a repeating pattern derived from the VM monogram.Applications:• Tissue paper• Shopping bags• Gift boxes• Website backgrounds• Premium stationery• Packaging interiors
Photography Style
Luxury editorial style.Soft natural lighting.Minimal backgrounds.Neutral colour palette.High-resolution imagery.Focus on craftsmanship and product detail.
Iconography
Use a consistent outline icon family.Rounded corners.Minimal styling.24px base grid.Consistent stroke width throughout the interface.
Motion Principles
Elegant and restrained.Soft fades.Smooth page transitions.Subtle hover animations.No excessive motion or distracting effects.
Accessibility
Maintain WCAG 2.2 AA contrast.Ensure logo remains legible in dark and light themes.Provide monochrome versions where required.
