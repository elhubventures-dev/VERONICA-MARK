
VERONICA MARK
Brand Guidelines — Volume IV
Brand Applications
Introduction
This volume defines how the VERONICA MARK identity is consistently applied across physical products, printed materials and digital touchpoints.
Packaging System
Luxury rigid gift box, premium shopping bag, cream tissue paper with VM pattern, champagne gold foil seal, thank-you card, authenticity certificate, gift ribbon and branded shipping carton.
Stationery
Business cards, letterheads, envelopes, invoices, receipts, quotation templates, presentation folders and email signatures using the official logo, colors and typography.
Retail & Delivery
Delivery vehicle branding, courier uniforms, staff ID cards, exhibition booths, retail signage, pull-up banners and window graphics.
Product Branding
Perfume labels, bottle boxes, hang tags, warranty/authenticity cards, QR authentication labels and care cards.
Corporate Identity
Company profile, investor presentation, press kit, media kit and official document templates.
Digital Applications
Website favicon, app icon, loading screen, splash screen, social avatars, email templates, web banners and digital advertisements.
Social Media
Profile images, cover banners, Instagram templates, Facebook creatives, LinkedIn banners, Pinterest pins, story templates and campaign layouts.
Photography & Mockups
Use premium lifestyle mockups, minimal backgrounds, consistent lighting and luxury presentation for every branded application.
Production Standards
Use vector artwork (SVG, AI, EPS) for print. CMYK for print, RGB for digital. Minimum print resolution 300 DPI. Protect clear space and never alter logo proportions or brand colors.
Quality Control
Every external asset must be reviewed against the official brand colors (#4B246A, #F8F4EC, #1A1A1A, #C7A25A), typography, spacing and logo usage rules before publication.
